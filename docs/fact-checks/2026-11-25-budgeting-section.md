Independent check of:

I re-read the live official pages on 2026-11-25 with WebFetch. Edmonton's page returned 502, so I confirmed it through edmonton.ca search results. I read the Soman & Cheema PDF and the Madrian & Shea abstract directly. Olson et al. 2023 is behind a paywall, so I used the Kellogg Insight summary and the journal index. I did not re-read Thaler & Benartzi 2004 this session; it is taken from the research notes.

Verdicts: CONFIRMED / WRONG / UNSUPPORTED / MISLEADING. Six items need a fix before publishing: #3, #8, #19, #26, #33 and #34. These are the items where a reader could lose money.
