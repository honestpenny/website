## Summary

- **WRONG (2):** "the most an employee pays in base CPP in 2027 is $4,209" (that's base + first additional; base alone is $3,477), and
  "higher earners pay a little more CPP" (the base rate falls from 4.95% to 4.75% on Jan 1, 2027, so every employee pays *less* CPP on the
  same pay; max CPP falls $4,230.45 → $4,209.00). The fact label `cpp-max-employee-2027` is wrong for the same reason.
- **MISLEADING (9)** and **UNSUPPORTED (1)**: listed below with exact fixes.
- **MISSING (5)**: things a reader needs to avoid a costly mistake (listed at the end).
- Everything else, including the full 2027 combined-rate table, the $66,000 inflation figure and the $10,000 worked example, is CONFIRMED.
