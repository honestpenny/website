# Federal news scan: 2026-11-11 to 2026-11-25

Scanned 2026-11-25. Official sources only. The scan covered the canada.ca news API feed for all departments from 2026-11-12 to 11-25, plus department pages: Finance, CRA, ESDC, Competition Bureau, FCAC, ISED, Bank of Canada, OSFI, Statistics Canada The Daily, parl.ca LEGISinfo and the CRA benefit calendar.

Score = usefulness to Honest Penny readers (1 = low, 5 = high).

## Confirmed items, ranked

### 1. Bill C-42 passes the House: one-time Groceries and Essentials Benefit supplement in early 2027 (score 5)
- **Dates:** Finance tabled it on 2026-11-16. LEGISinfo shows first reading 11-18 and all House stages done on 11-20. Senate first reading was 11-24. The Senate has set a Committee of the Whole for 2026-12-03.
- **URLs:**
  - https://www.canada.ca/en/department-finance/news/2026/11/government-of-canada-tables-plan-to-ease-grocery-costs-support-homeownership-and-encourage-new-home-construction.html
  - https://www.parl.ca/legisinfo/en/bill/45-1/c-42
- **What changes:** The bill raises the maximum Canada Groceries and Essentials Benefit by 50% for the 2026-27 benefit year. Finance says this means up to $339 for a single person with no children and up to $679 for a couple with two children, paid once in "early 2027". No exact date has been announced. The regular CGEB payment is 2027-01-05 (CRA benefit calendar). The bill also extends the foreign-buyer ban on existing homes to 2029-01-01, when the Act would be repealed. It is **not law yet**, because the Senate hasn't passed it.
- **Guides affected:** budgeting and 8 Steps, if they mention benefits. It could be a short new explainer: "Will I get the grocery supplement, and how much?"

### 2. October 2026 CPI: inflation at 3.1%, gasoline up 30.9% year over year (score 4)
- **Date:** 2026-11-16
- **URL:** https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/n1/daily-quotidien/261116/dq261116a-eng.htm
- **What changes:** Headline CPI was 3.1% year over year, the same as September. Excluding energy it was 2.0%. Gasoline was up 30.9% (StatCan cites a base-year effect), and groceries bought in stores were up 2.0% (1.7% in September). Cellular services were down 6.2% year over year. Shelter was up 1.4%, and rent growth slowed. The page I read gave no Alberta-specific figure.
- **Guides affected:** budgeting. Phones/internet can cite the 6.2% drop in cell prices. Car buying covers fuel costs. This isn't a reason to change anything in the 2027 bracket guide, because indexation uses earlier CPI data.

### 3. CRA January 2027 payroll formulas (T4127, 124th ed.) confirm 2027 federal and Alberta brackets (score 4)
- **Date:** revision "Rev. 27 (26/11)", so November 2026. I couldn't confirm the exact day.
- **URL:** https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/payroll/t4127-payroll-deductions-formulas/t4127-jan/t4127-jan-payroll-deductions-formulas-computer-programs.html
- **What changes:**
  - Federal: indexed by 2.6%. Thresholds are $60,044 / $120,088 / $186,157 / $265,203, with rates of 14 / 20.5 / 26 / 29 / 33%. The basic personal amount is $16,879 (falling to $15,214 at high incomes).
  - Alberta: indexed by 2.0% (capped by the Alberta escalator). Thresholds are $62,424 / $157,344 / $188,813 / $251,750 / $377,625, with rates of 8 / 10 / 12 / 13 / 14 / 15%. The basic personal amount is $23,225.
  - All of this takes effect on 2027-01-01.
- **Guides affected:** 2027 tax brackets. I checked `alberta-income-tax-brackets-2027.mdx` and it already uses these figures (2.6%, 2.0%, $60,044, $62,424). No change is needed, but re-verify the BPA figures if the guide states them.

### 4. Bank of Canada renews the 2% inflation target for 2027-2031 (score 3)
- **Date:** 2026-11-24
- **URL:** https://www.bankofcanada.ca/core-functions/monetary-policy/renewing-canadas-monetary-policy-framework/2027-2031-monetary-policy-framework-governing-councils-statement/
- **What changes:** The target stays at 2% with a 1-3% range, running January 2027 to December 2031. The Bank says it will be more alert to large or persistent supply shocks. CPI-trim and CPIX become its main core measures, and it will publish a new inflation dashboard. Separately, on 2026-11-16, the Bank announced that Governor Macklem will step down in June 2027 (https://www.bankofcanada.ca/2026/11/bank-of-canada-governor-tiff-macklem-to-step-down-in-june-2027/). The next rate decision is **2026-12-09**.
- **Guides affected:** none directly. It's useful context for bank accounts (savings rates) and mortgages.

### 5. $5,000 Red Seal Completion Bonus: first payments the first week of December (score 3)

### 6. Competition Bureau gets a court order in its Old Navy and Gap marketing investigation (score 3)
- **Date:** 2026-11-18
- **URL:** https://www.canada.ca/en/competition-bureau/news/2026/11/old-navy-and-gap-marketing-practices-investigation-to-advance-upon-court-order.html
- **What changes:** The Bureau is investigating:
  - whether the "regular" prices behind sales are genuine
  - countdown timers
  - "Only a few left" and "Selling fast" messages
  - "X people viewing" counts
  - "Price drop" and "Best deal in 30 days" claims

  There has been no finding of wrongdoing. It is a good, concrete example of fake urgency to warn readers about before Black Friday.
- **Guides affected:** budgeting. It also fits car buying if that guide covers pressure tactics.

### 7. Consumer-driven ("open") banking: standards body and complaints body named (score 2)
- **Date:** the release text says 2026-11-20. The feed timestamp is 11-19.
- **URL:** https://www.canada.ca/en/department-finance/news/2026/11/canada-takes-next-steps-in-implementing-the-consumer-driven-banking-framework.html
- **What changes:**
  - FDX (with a Canada Council) will set the technical standard.
  - OBSI will handle complaints that aren't resolved.
  - The Bank of Canada will supervise and keep a public registry of accredited providers.
  - Final regulations will come into force in stages. **No launch date is given.**
  - About 9 million Canadians share bank data through screen scraping today.
- **Guides affected:** bank accounts, as a future note on budgeting apps and screen scraping.
