### 1. Bill 41 (Care-First auto insurance amendments) passed third reading. Score 5
- **Date:** introduced 2026-11-17; second reading 2026-11-23; Committee of the Whole 2026-11-24; third reading passed 2026-11-25 (afternoon). Royal Assent not yet recorded.
- **Official URLs:**
  - Bill status report, "Activity to Wednesday, November 25, 2026": https://www.assembly.ab.ca/LAO/Bills/bsr31-2.pdf
  - Government release "More choice, more certainty" (2026-11-17): https://www.alberta.ca/announcements.cfm?xID=98152307CC832-0497-C4E1-56376B0A4C28F5AD
- **Summary:** Bill 41, the *Financial Statutes Amendment Act, 2026 (No. 2)*, makes the last law changes (across seven insurance acts) that Care-First auto insurance needs to start on **January 1, 2027**. Private insurers will still sell the policies. The bill also lets retirees in some workplace pension plans choose Variable Life Benefits (money stays invested and pays a flexible monthly income) and make voluntary contributions. It also allows business "captive" insurers, gives the Alberta Securities Commission a dispute route for shareholders, and keeps the smokeless tobacco tax at 35 cents per gram.
- **Why it matters:** This removes the "will the law be in place on Jan 1?" doubt. Royal Assent is still to come (it is usually a formality).
- **Affects:** car/home insurance Care-First guide; Care-First renewal checker. Pension items could later go into the 8 Steps or a retirement guide.

### 2. AIRB publishes "Care-First: Your Premium Questions Answered". Score 5
- **Date:** published 2026-11-25 (page metadata says datePublished 2026-11-25T16:15Z, modified 19:18Z).
- **Official URL:** https://www.airbfordrivers.ca/care-first-your-premium-questions-answered/
- **Summary:** Your policy's **effective (renewal) date** decides which pricing you get, not the date the renewal notice arrives. A December 2026 renewal is on current rates, and a March 2027 renewal is on Care-First rates. Insurers send notices 45 to 60 days before renewal. AIRB-approved filings average **-13.7% (about $297 a year) for private passenger vehicles**, with the biggest drops for younger drivers. **Motorcycles average about +100%.** Commercial vehicles vary. These are averages, not guarantees, and higher repair and parts costs can eat into the savings. From 2027, an **Adjusted Rate Cap** replaces the Good Driver Rate Cap: insurers can raise overall rates by at most 5% a year, and the insurer's rate change alone can raise your premium by at most 10% at renewal. The 10% cap does not cover changes in your own risk, and it does not apply to 2027 renewals priced under Care-First. The page says nothing about pink cards or liability cards.
- **Related, also official:** "Understanding Alberta auto insurance rate caps" (published 2026-11-04, updated 2026-11-24): https://www.airbfordrivers.ca/articles/understanding-alberta-auto-insurance-rate-caps/ . The AIRB resource "Final Care-First Actuarial Costing Report" was updated on 2026-11-25 (https://www.airbfordrivers.ca/resources/final-care-first-actuarial-costing-report/), but I could not read its contents. Read it before using it.
- **Affects:** car/home insurance Care-First guide; Care-First renewal checker (check the effective-date rule, the motorcycle +100% figure, the 45 to 60 day notice window, and that the 10% cap does not apply to 2027 Care-First renewals).

### 3. Calgary's proposed 2027–2030 budget: 15% municipal tax revenue increase for 2027 (not yet approved). Score 5

- **Summary:** The proposal needs a **15% increase in municipal property tax revenue from existing properties in 2027**. A planned 0.25-point shift of tax share from non-residential to residential properties also starts in 2027.
- **Affects:** property assessment guide (Calgary tax context); budgeting guide (a line for housing costs). Re-check after Council votes in December.

### 4. Bill 34 consumer protections passed third reading: lemon law, credit freezes, right to repair, all-in ticket resale pricing. Score 4
- **Date:** introduced 2026-10-28; third reading passed **2026-11-18**. Royal Assent not yet recorded. Changes take effect "upon proclamation"; the government targets early 2027 for the regulations.
- **Official URLs:**
  - https://www.alberta.ca/protecting-consumers-and-property-rights
  - Bill status: https://www.assembly.ab.ca/LAO/Bills/bsr31-2.pdf
- **Summary:** The bill amends the *Consumer Protection Act*. **Lemon law:** buyers or lessees can sue over a vehicle that can't be fixed, within 3 years or 60,000 km, after 3 failed repairs of the same warranty defect, or 12 failed repairs across several defects, or more than 30 days out of service for warranty repair. **Credit freezes:** Albertans could freeze or restrict access to their credit reports. **Right to repair:** a framework for access to parts, tools and repair information, with the products to be named in regulation. **Ticket resale:** resale sellers and platforms must show the total price. The bill also includes a 12-month pilot selling lottery tickets in 30 liquor stores.
- **Affects:** car buying guide (lemon-law rights once in force); 8 Steps or budgeting (a credit freeze against identity theft); possibly a future scams guide. Do not tell readers these rights exist yet. They do not until proclamation.

### 7. Natural gas: no rebate for November; the trigger is still $6.50/GJ; winter disconnection rules are in effect. Score 3
- **Official URLs:**
  - https://www.alberta.ca/natural-gas-rebate-program
  - https://ucahelps.alberta.ca/utility-choices/current-rates-and-pricing/default-rates/
  - https://www.auc.ab.ca/winter-utility-reconnection-program/
- **Summary:** The Natural Gas Rebate Program pays only when the "Alberta price" (the highest regulated monthly rate among ATCO North, ATCO South and Apex) goes above **$6.50/GJ**. The **November 2026 Alberta price is $1.99/GJ**, so there is no rebate for November. The UCA lists the November regulated rates at 1.893 and 1.993 $/GJ. The December 2026 rate was not posted at scan time. Under AUC Rule 003, utilities cannot disconnect **electricity from Oct 15 to Apr 15** or **natural gas from Nov 1 to Apr 15**, or at any time when the forecast is below 0°C for the next 24 hours. The UCA runs the Winter Utility Reconnection Program (310-4822). People already disconnected, or due to be within 5 days, can mark a provincial emergency benefits application as urgent.
- **Affects:** natural gas guide (a December rate update is due; rebate is unlikely at these prices); electricity guide (disconnection rules).
